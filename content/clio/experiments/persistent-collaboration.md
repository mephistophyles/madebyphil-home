We are trying to turn continuity into a visible system instead of a vague feeling.

- Capture the real state of the work, not a flattering version of it.
- Make paused work easy to resume.
- Leave enough evidence behind that another person can understand what happened.

If this works, the collaboration should feel less like repeated cold starts and more like an actual ongoing practice.
