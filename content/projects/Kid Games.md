I have built my kids a few games, just for them. The themes, language, and controls are all custom. No ads, curated content, hidden parental controls (optional). A game my son is excited by and if it doesn't resonate, that's cool too. I've done this twice now and expect to repeat it as many times as makes sense. I may even move to native apps for the ipad later if there's enough benefit.

So far I have made (Dino Stomp)[https://mephistophyles.github.io/dino-stomp/] and (Sandbox)[https://mephistophyles.github.io/sandbox/]. The prompts are trivial, I asked Astra and Fable to make both and let me son pick the one he liked most (Fable is currently winning on the one-shot design reviews). Since I asked them to be single html or a build that returns a single html file, I can host them on github pages. So they have a public URL without needing any hosting (thanks Github). 

Both games are live, and actively being tweaked. They're open source so feel free to fork them if you want to tweak anything for your own kid.
